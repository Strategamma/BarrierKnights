How to play
  • Reach the glowing end line opposite your starting side.
  • In bot matches, Gold starts at the bottom and moves upward.
  • Each action lets you move, place one complete wall, or draw a card.
  • Every wall must leave both players a path to their destination.
  • Land on W for a wall, + for an action, or ? for a free card effect.
  • Pawns may share a square but cannot jump. Cards activate immediately.
  • On touch screens, preview a wall and confirm it before placement.
